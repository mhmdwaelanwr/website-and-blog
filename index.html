HOW I WORK

Engineering habits I try to make visible in the code.

01

Evidence before assumptions

Reproduce, instrument, isolate, and document the failure mode before optimizing the fix.

02

Human authority around AI

AI can propose and accelerate; identity, publication, and consequential actions should have explicit review boundaries.

03

Ship the operational path

Packaging, recovery, configuration, diagnostics, CI, and documentation are part of the product — not cleanup after the feature.

04

Understand below the surface

I prefer root-cause investigation over a workaround when the system gives enough evidence to learn what is actually happening.
